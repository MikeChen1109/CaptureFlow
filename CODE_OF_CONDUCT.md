# Code of Conduct

CaptureFlow contributors are expected to keep discussion practical, respectful, and focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Assume contributors are acting in good faith.
- Give technical feedback with concrete examples.
- Respect different skill levels and backgrounds.
- Keep disagreements centered on implementation, tradeoffs, and project goals.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Publishing private information without permission
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussion after maintainers ask to move on

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or limit participation when behavior harms the project. If you need to report a problem, contact the maintainers through the repository's published contact channel.
